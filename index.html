Trend lines, circles, and channels

Fib channels

Two channels share one direction. Level 0 is the kept trend line. Red, green, blue, aqua, violet, and white are 0.236, 0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 0.786, and 1. The dashed channel is the one built off the purple 0.618.

Fib circles

Each circle’s handles are the squares on a kept trend line. That line is the center. Rings that price kept hitting stay bright. The next ring out is faint.

Lines that only mattered in a crash, or that cut through price without repeated support and resistance, were left off. Color is only so the lines can be told apart.
