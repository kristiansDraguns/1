Fib channel blocks

How it is built

Filled fib-channel blocks in two directions. The rising stack follows the rising support. The flat stack follows the ceiling. A new channel is lined up so its levels land on the channel already there. The cells where they cross are the quadrants. The white circle and the blue circle each run from one crossing to the next.
