Swing chart

How it is built

Two filled fib-channel directions. The rising ones follow the cycle floor and the rising support. The flat one follows the ceiling. Each is built upward from the cyan 0.618, with a back channel under the base. The light lines mark the base, the 0.618, and the blue rail, the levels he keeps when the chart gets busy. The orange circle runs from one crossing on the yellow 0.5 line to the next crossing on that same line.
